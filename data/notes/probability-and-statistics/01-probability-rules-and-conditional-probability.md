# Probability Rules and Conditional Probability

**Definition:** Probability assigns values from 0 to 1 to events, and conditional probability describes the chance of an event given that another event occurred.

## Key points
- For complementary events A and not A, their probabilities sum to 1.
- For independent events, learning that one occurred does not change the probability of the other.

## Example
If a fair coin is tossed, the chance of heads is 1/2; the chance of two heads in two independent tosses is 1/4.
