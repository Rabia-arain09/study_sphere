# Gradients and Directional Derivatives

**Definition:** The gradient collects a function's partial derivatives, and a directional derivative measures change along a chosen direction.

## Key points
- The gradient points toward the direction of greatest local increase for a differentiable function.
- The directional derivative is the dot product of the gradient and a unit direction vector.

## Example
For f(x,y)=x+y, the gradient is (1,1), so the rate along a unit vector u is (1,1) dot u.
