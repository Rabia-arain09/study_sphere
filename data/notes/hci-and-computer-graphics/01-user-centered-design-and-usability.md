# User-Centered Design and Usability

**Definition:** User-centered design involves users throughout design, and usability describes how effectively, efficiently, and satisfactorily they can achieve goals with a product.

## Key points
- Start by understanding users, their tasks, and the context in which they work.
- Iterative prototypes let a team test ideas and improve them using feedback.

## Example
A team can sketch a booking screen, ask users to complete a task, and revise confusing controls.
