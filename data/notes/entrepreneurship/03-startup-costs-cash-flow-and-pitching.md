# Startup Costs, Cash Flow, and Pitching

**Definition:** Startup finance estimates the money needed to begin and operate an idea, while cash flow tracks money entering and leaving over time.

## Key points
- Separate one-time setup costs from recurring expenses and expected income.
- A concise pitch explains the customer problem, proposed value, evidence, and next step.

## Example
If a service has monthly costs of 500 and earns 10 per subscriber, it needs 50 subscribers per month to cover those costs.
