# Management Functions and Organization

**Definition:** Management coordinates people and resources through functions such as planning, organizing, leading, and controlling.

## Key points
- Organizational structure clarifies roles and reporting relationships.
- Managers coordinate work toward shared goals rather than carrying out every task themselves.

## Example
A restaurant manager schedules staff, assigns stations, and checks that service meets its goals.
