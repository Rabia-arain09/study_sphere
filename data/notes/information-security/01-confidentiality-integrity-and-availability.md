# Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability

**Definition:** The CIA triad summarizes three security goals: keeping information private, accurate, and accessible to authorized users.

## Key points
- Confidentiality limits disclosure; integrity protects against improper change; availability supports timely access.
- A security control may improve one goal while requiring trade-offs with another.

## Example
A school protects grades with access permissions, change logs, and reliable backups.
