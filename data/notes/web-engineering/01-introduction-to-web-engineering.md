# Introduction to Web Engineering

*Web Engineering · Semester 5 · Week 1 · Difficulty 1/5*

**Key concept:** Web engineering applies planning, methodology and quality control to build large web applications that stay reliable as they grow.

## How it works
- Web systems change quickly, serve many users and must be available all the time.
- A disciplined **process** covers requirements, design, development, testing and maintenance.
- **Quality attributes:** usability, performance, security, scalability, maintainability.
- Without engineering, sites become hard to change and break under load.

## Example

An online shop expects 100 visitors a day but a sale brings 10,000. A scalable design (load balancing, caching) was planned for this, while an ad-hoc site goes down.
