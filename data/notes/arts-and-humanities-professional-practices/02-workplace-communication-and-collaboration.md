# Workplace Communication and Collaboration

**Definition:** Professional communication shares information clearly and respectfully, while collaboration coordinates people working toward a common result.

## Key points
- Choose a communication method that fits the message, audience, and urgency.
- Listen, document decisions, and clarify ownership of follow-up tasks.

## Example
After a meeting, a team member sends a short summary listing decisions, owners, and due dates.
