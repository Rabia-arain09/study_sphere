# Paragraph Structure and Coherence

**Definition:** A paragraph develops one main idea with related supporting sentences in a clear order.

## Key points
- A topic sentence introduces the focus; supporting details explain or prove it.
- Transitions show how ideas relate and help a reader follow the sequence.

## Example
A paragraph about exercise can begin with its benefit, give two examples, and end with a sentence linking them to health.
