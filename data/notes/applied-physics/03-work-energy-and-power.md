# Work, Energy, and Power

**Definition:** Work transfers energy when a force causes displacement, and power measures how quickly work is done.

## Key points
- Kinetic energy depends on mass and speed; gravitational potential energy depends on height.
- In an ideal closed system, total energy is conserved even as it changes form.

## Example
Lifting a book increases its gravitational potential energy; doing the lift faster requires more power.
