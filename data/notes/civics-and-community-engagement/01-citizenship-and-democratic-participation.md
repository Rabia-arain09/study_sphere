# Citizenship and Democratic Participation

**Definition:** Civic participation is involvement in public decisions and community life through lawful and constructive activities.

## Key points
- Participation can include voting where eligible, public consultation, volunteering, and informed discussion.
- Respectful engagement includes listening to people with different experiences.

## Example
Residents can attend a local meeting and present evidence about a safety concern on their street.
