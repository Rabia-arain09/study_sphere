# Reading for Main Ideas and Evidence

**Definition:** The main idea is the central point of a text, and evidence is the detail that supports or explains that point.

## Key points
- Headings, topic sentences, and repeated ideas can help locate the main idea.
- Separate a writer's claim from the facts or examples used to support it.

## Example
In a paragraph about saving water, the main idea may be that small daily habits reduce waste; turning off a tap is supporting evidence.
