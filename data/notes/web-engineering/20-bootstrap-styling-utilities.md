# Bootstrap Styling Utilities

*Web Engineering · Semester 5 · Week 2-3 · Difficulty 2/5*

**Key concept:** Utility classes apply common styles directly in HTML without writing CSS.

## How it works
- **Spacing:** m-3, p-2, mt-4, px-3 (scale 0-5).
- **Typography:** text-center, fw-bold, fs-3, text-primary.
- **Flexbox:** d-flex, justify-content-between, align-items-center.
- **Borders and shadows:** border, rounded, shadow.
- **Position and sizing:** position-relative, w-100, h-50.

## Example

A centered, spaced row of items with a shadow:

```html
<div class="d-flex justify-content-between align-items-center p-3 shadow rounded">...</div>
```
