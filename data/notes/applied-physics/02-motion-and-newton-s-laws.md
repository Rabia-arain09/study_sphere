# Motion and Newton's Laws

**Definition:** Kinematics describes motion using position, velocity, and acceleration, while Newton's laws relate forces to changes in motion.

## Key points
- Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity over time.
- For a constant mass, the net force is mass multiplied by acceleration: F = ma.

## Example
A 2 kg cart with a net force of 6 N accelerates at 3 m/s^2.
