# Networks and Digital Safety

**Definition:** A computer network connects devices so they can exchange data and share services.

## Key points
- Use trusted, encrypted connections for sensitive activity and keep software updated.
- Check unexpected links and requests carefully; phishing messages try to trick people into sharing information.

## Example
Before entering a password, a student checks that the school website address is correct and uses HTTPS.
