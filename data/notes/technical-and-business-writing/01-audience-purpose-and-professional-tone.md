# Audience, Purpose, and Professional Tone

**Definition:** Effective professional writing matches its detail, structure, and tone to the reader and the writer's purpose.

## Key points
- Identify what the audience already knows and what action or understanding is needed.
- Use direct, respectful language and explain technical terms the reader may not know.

## Example
A project update for executives can lead with status and risks, while a developer note can include implementation details.
