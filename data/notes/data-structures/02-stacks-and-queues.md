# Stacks and Queues

**Definition:** A stack removes the most recently added item first, while a queue removes the earliest added item first.

## Key points
- A stack uses push and pop operations and follows last-in, first-out order.
- A queue uses enqueue and dequeue operations and follows first-in, first-out order.

## Example
A browser back history can use a stack, while people waiting for service form a queue.
