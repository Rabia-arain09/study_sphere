# Differentiation and Rates of Change

**Definition:** The derivative measures the instantaneous rate of change of a function and gives the slope of its tangent line.

## Key points
- A derivative can be defined as the limit of average rates of change over smaller intervals.
- Derivative rules make it possible to differentiate sums, products, and common functions efficiently.

## Example
For f(x)=x^2, the derivative is 2x, so the slope at x=3 is 6.
