# Summaries and Paraphrases

**Definition:** A summary briefly states a text's main points, while a paraphrase restates a specific idea in new wording.

## Key points
- Both keep the source meaning accurate and use the writer's own words.
- Cite the source when using its ideas; changing a few words does not make copying acceptable.

## Example
A reader can reduce a five-sentence passage to one sentence that states its main point, then cite the passage.
