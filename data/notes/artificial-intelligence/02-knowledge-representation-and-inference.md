# Knowledge Representation and Inference

**Definition:** Knowledge representation stores facts and relationships in a form a program can reason about, while inference derives conclusions from them.

## Key points
- Rules can express patterns such as "if the conditions hold, then conclude a result."
- The conclusions are only as reliable as the facts, rules, and assumptions supplied.

## Example
Given "all mammals are warm-blooded" and "a whale is a mammal," a rule system can infer that a whale is warm-blooded.
