# Encryption, Hashing, and Digital Signatures

**Definition:** Encryption transforms data so only a holder of the appropriate key can recover it, hashing produces a fixed-size digest, and digital signatures help verify origin and integrity.

## Key points
- Symmetric encryption uses a shared secret key; public-key encryption uses a related public and private key pair.
- A cryptographic hash is not encryption and does not by itself hide the original data.

## Example
A recipient can verify a signed document with the sender's public key and compare its hash to detect changes.
