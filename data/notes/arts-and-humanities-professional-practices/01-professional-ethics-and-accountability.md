# Professional Ethics and Accountability

**Definition:** Professional ethics applies principles such as honesty, fairness, and respect to work decisions, and accountability means taking responsibility for those decisions.

## Key points
- Recognize conflicts of interest and disclose them when relevant.
- Follow professional rules and correct mistakes rather than concealing them.

## Example
A designer who notices a serious error in a report informs the team and helps correct it before release.
