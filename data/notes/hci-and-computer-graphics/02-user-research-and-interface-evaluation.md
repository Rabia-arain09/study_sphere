# User Research and Interface Evaluation

**Definition:** User research gathers evidence about people and their needs, while interface evaluation checks how well a design supports those needs.

## Key points
- Interviews explore experiences; usability tests observe people trying realistic tasks.
- Use clear measures such as task success and observed difficulty, and avoid leading participants.

## Example
A researcher watches several users try to find a setting and records where they get stuck.
