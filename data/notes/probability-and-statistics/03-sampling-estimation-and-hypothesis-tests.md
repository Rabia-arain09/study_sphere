# Sampling, Estimation, and Hypothesis Tests

**Definition:** Statistical inference uses sample data to estimate population properties or assess a claim about a population.

## Key points
- A confidence interval describes a range produced by a method with a stated long-run coverage level.
- A hypothesis test evaluates sample evidence under a null model; a small p-value is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true.

## Example
A poll of a random sample can estimate support in a larger population, with uncertainty reported in an interval.
