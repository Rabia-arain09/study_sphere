# Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

**Definition:** An eigenvector of a square matrix is a nonzero vector whose direction is unchanged by the matrix transformation, and its eigenvalue is the scale factor.

## Key points
- For a matrix A and eigenvector v, the defining equation is Av=lambda v.
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors help describe important directions of a linear transformation.

## Example
If A doubles every vector, every nonzero vector is an eigenvector with eigenvalue 2.
