# Community Needs Assessment

**Definition:** A community needs assessment gathers information about local conditions and priorities before selecting an action.

## Key points
- Use more than one suitable source, such as interviews, observation, or public data.
- Include the perspectives of the people affected and protect their privacy.

## Example
A student group can ask residents about access to a park before proposing improvements.
