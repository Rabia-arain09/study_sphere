# Business Models and Customer Validation

**Definition:** A business model describes how an organization creates, delivers, and captures value, and customer validation tests its assumptions with potential users.

## Key points
- A model identifies customers, channels, key activities, and likely costs or revenue sources.
- Small interviews or prototypes can test demand before large investments are made.

## Example
A team can show a clickable service prototype to commuters and record whether it solves their stated problem.
