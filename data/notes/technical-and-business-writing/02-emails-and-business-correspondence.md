# Emails and Business Correspondence

**Definition:** Professional correspondence communicates a clear message using an appropriate subject, concise structure, and courteous tone.

## Key points
- A useful email subject summarizes the message or requested action.
- Organize the message so the main point and next steps are easy to find.

## Example
An email requesting a review can state the deadline, link the document, and name the feedback needed.
