# Coordinate Geometry and Conic Sections

**Definition:** Coordinate geometry represents points and curves with equations, and conic sections are curves formed by intersecting a plane with a cone.

## Key points
- The distance and midpoint formulas connect coordinates to geometric measurements.
- Circles, parabolas, ellipses, and hyperbolas can be described by standard equations.

## Example
The equation (x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=9 describes a circle centered at (1,-2) with radius 3.
