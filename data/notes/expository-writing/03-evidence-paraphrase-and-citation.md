# Evidence, Paraphrase, and Citation

**Definition:** Evidence supports an explanation, paraphrasing restates a source idea in new words, and citation identifies that source.

## Key points
- Choose relevant, credible sources and represent their meaning accurately.
- Cite borrowed ideas as well as direct quotations to distinguish source material from your own analysis.

## Example
A writer can restate a report's finding in original wording and cite the report after the sentence.
