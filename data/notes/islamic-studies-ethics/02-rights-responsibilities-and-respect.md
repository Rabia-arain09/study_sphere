# Rights, Responsibilities, and Respect

**Definition:** A right is a justified freedom or protection, and a responsibility is a duty to act with care toward others.

## Key points
- Rights and responsibilities work together in families, schools, and communities.
- Respectful disagreement allows people to discuss ideas without denying another person's dignity.
