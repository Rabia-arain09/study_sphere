# Planning, Decision Making, and Leadership

**Definition:** Planning sets objectives and actions, decision making selects among alternatives, and leadership guides people toward shared goals.

## Key points
- A useful plan connects objectives to resources, responsibilities, and timelines.
- Decisions should consider evidence, constraints, and possible consequences.

## Example
A team lead compares two delivery schedules and chooses one that meets the deadline with available staff.
