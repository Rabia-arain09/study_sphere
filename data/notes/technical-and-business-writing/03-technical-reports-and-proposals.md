# Technical Reports and Proposals

**Definition:** A technical report presents a question, method, evidence, and findings, while a proposal recommends planned work and explains its value.

## Key points
- Headings and visuals help readers locate important information.
- Separate evidence from recommendations and support claims with reliable sources.

## Example
A proposal for replacing old lab computers can compare options, costs, and expected benefits.
