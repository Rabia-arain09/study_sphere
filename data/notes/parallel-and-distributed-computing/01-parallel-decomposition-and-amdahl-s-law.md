# Parallel Decomposition and Amdahl's Law

**Definition:** Parallel computing divides work among processing units, and Amdahl's law estimates speedup when part of a task must remain serial.

## Key points
- Independent tasks can often run at the same time on different processors.
- A serial fraction limits the maximum speedup no matter how many processors are added.

## Example
If 10 percent of a program is serial, adding processors cannot make that portion execute in parallel.
