# Motivation, Teamwork, and Performance

**Definition:** Motivation influences the effort people choose to give, while teamwork coordinates their skills and performance management tracks progress toward goals.

## Key points
- Clear expectations and constructive feedback help people understand how to contribute.
- Useful performance measures should relate to goals and be interpreted in context.

## Example
A supervisor can set a clear service target, review progress with the team, and agree on support where needed.
