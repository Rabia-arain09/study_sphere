# Double Integrals and Accumulation

**Definition:** A double integral adds values of a function over a two-dimensional region and can measure area-weighted accumulation or volume.

## Key points
- The limits of integration describe the region over which values are accumulated.
- For a nonnegative surface, a double integral gives the volume under it above the region.

## Example
Integrating a constant height of 2 over a rectangle of area 3 gives volume 6.
