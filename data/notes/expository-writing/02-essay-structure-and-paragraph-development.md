# Essay Structure and Paragraph Development

**Definition:** An expository essay organizes an introduction, connected body paragraphs, and a conclusion to explain its thesis.

## Key points
- Each body paragraph should develop one supporting idea with evidence and explanation.
- Transitions make the relationships between ideas clear to the reader.

## Example
A paragraph can state one benefit of recycling, give a school example, and explain how it supports the thesis.
