# Project Proposal, Planning, and Research Ethics

**Definition:** A project proposal explains the problem, objectives, approach, and plan, while research ethics protects people and data involved in the work.

## Key points
- A plan breaks work into tasks with milestones and identifies risks or dependencies.
- Get appropriate consent, protect private information, and credit sources and collaborators.

## Example
A proposal for user testing can schedule a pilot and explain how participant feedback will be recorded without unnecessary personal data.
