# Intellectual Property and Data Privacy

**Definition:** Intellectual property concerns rights in creative or technical work, and data privacy concerns appropriate handling of information about people.

## Key points
- Credit sources and check permission or license terms before reusing work.
- Collect and share only personal information needed for a legitimate task, and protect it from unauthorized access.

## Example
A project report can cite an image source and remove personal identifiers from a sample dataset.
