# Purpose, Audience, and Thesis

**Definition:** Expository writing explains a subject clearly, and a thesis states the main point the writing will develop.

## Key points
- A writer should match detail, vocabulary, and tone to the intended audience.
- A focused thesis gives the reader a clear guide to the explanation.

## Example
For an essay explaining recycling at school, a thesis can state that clear sorting stations reduce waste.
