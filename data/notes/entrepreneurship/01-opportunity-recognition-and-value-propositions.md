# Opportunity Recognition and Value Propositions

**Definition:** An entrepreneurial opportunity is a problem or unmet need that can be addressed with a feasible offering, and a value proposition explains its benefit to a customer.

## Key points
- A strong opportunity is grounded in evidence about a real group of users.
- A value proposition states who benefits, what need is met, and why the offering is useful.

## Example
A student team may discover that commuters need reliable campus bus updates and propose a simple alert service.
