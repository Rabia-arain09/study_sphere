# Ethical Principles and Moral Reasoning

**Definition:** Ethical reasoning uses values, duties, and likely effects to decide what action is responsible and fair.

## Key points
- Honesty, fairness, and respect help people explain and evaluate choices.
- A sound decision considers who may be affected and whether the same standard is applied consistently.

## Example
If a shop gives a customer too much change, the customer can return the extra amount rather than keep it.
