# Planning and Intelligent Agents

**Definition:** An intelligent agent observes its environment and selects actions, while planning searches for an action sequence that can reach a goal.

## Key points
- An agent's sensors provide observations and its actuators or outputs affect the environment.
- A plan depends on the available actions, current state, and goal conditions.

## Example
A delivery robot can plan a route from its current location to a room while avoiding blocked corridors.
