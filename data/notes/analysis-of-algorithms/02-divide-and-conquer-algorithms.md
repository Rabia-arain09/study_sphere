# Divide-and-Conquer Algorithms

**Definition:** Divide and conquer solves a problem by splitting it into smaller subproblems, solving them, and combining their results.

## Key points
- The subproblems are often similar to the original problem but smaller.
- The recurrence relation can describe how the algorithm divides its work.

## Example
Merge sort divides a list in half, sorts each half, and merges the sorted halves.
